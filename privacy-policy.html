Life OS Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Life OS ("Life OS", "we", "our", or "us") is a productivity application designed to help you organize tasks, notes, goals, calendar information, habits, and other aspects of your daily life.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name and email address used for authentication and your profile.
  • User content: information you create in the app, such as tasks, notes, goals, calendar items, habits, and related productivity content.
  • Fitness information: step-count or pedometer information when you use the fitness/pedometer feature.
  • Device and advertising information: device identifiers, advertising-related information, and product interaction information used by third-party advertising services.
  • Diagnostics and performance information: crash and performance information that may be collected by third-party SDKs to help diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.

2. How We Use Information

  • create and maintain your account;
  • provide Life OS features and personalize your experience within the app;
  • save and display the productivity content you create;
  • provide pedometer and fitness-related features;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the application; and
  • display and measure third-party advertising.

3. Authentication and Storage

Life OS uses Firebase Authentication for account authentication. Some application information may be stored locally on your device, and information required for account-based features may be processed by our application services.

4. Advertising

Life OS uses third-party advertising services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), to display advertisements. These services may process information such as device identifiers, advertising information, product interactions, IP address or general location information, and diagnostic or performance information as described in their own privacy documentation.

Third-party advertising providers may use information for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and related advertising-service purposes. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Fitness and Pedometer Data

Life OS may use step-count or related fitness information to provide its pedometer and productivity features. We do not use fitness information for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate Life OS, including authentication, application infrastructure, diagnostics, performance services, and advertising services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the relevant Life OS features, maintain your account, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us through the Life OS project repository.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Life OS. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Life OS is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Third-Party Services

Life OS may use third-party services such as Firebase and Google Mobile Ads. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. You should review their policies for more information about their practices.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the Life OS project repository: github.com/tanvirnijjar/Life-OS
