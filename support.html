Life OS Support

Help and support for Life OS.

Need Help?

If you have a problem with Life OS, please provide a clear description of the issue, the feature you were using, and the steps that caused the problem.

Common Issues

  • Account: Make sure you are using the correct email address and account credentials.
  • Tasks, notes, goals, calendar, and habits: Check that you are signed in to the correct account and that the app has finished saving your changes.
  • Pedometer: Make sure Motion & Fitness access is allowed when requested by the app.

Privacy

For information about how Life OS handles data, please read our Privacy Policy.

Contact

For support, please open an issue in the Life OS GitHub repository with details about the problem.
